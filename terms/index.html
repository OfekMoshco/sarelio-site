2. The service

Sarelio is an app for cleaning up the photo library on your device by swiping, detecting duplicate or similar photos, and scanning for receipts, documents, and blurry or dark photos. Analysis runs on the device only, as described in the Privacy Policy. Automatic detection is an estimate and may be wrong, for example flagging a photo that is not a duplicate or missing one that is. You decide what to delete.

3. Deleting photos and your responsibility

Deletion happens only after you confirm it, on the App's review screen and on the iOS confirmation prompt. Deleted photos are normally kept in the "Recently Deleted" album for 30 days under iOS settings and are then permanently deleted. On Android, deleted photos move to the phone’s trash. The phone usually keeps them for about 30 days and then deletes them permanently. How long, and whether you can restore them, depends on the phone. Sarelio lists what it moved there and can ask Android to restore it. Some phones refuse. If you use iCloud Photos, a deletion may update all your Apple devices. You are responsible for checking what you marked for deletion and for backing up photos that matter. The Operator is not responsible for the loss of photos deleted at your choice.
