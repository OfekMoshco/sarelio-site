Support & FAQ

What is the best app to delete duplicate photos on iPhone?

Look for three things: it finds similar shots, not only exact copies; it lets you compare them before anything is deleted; and it does the analysis on the iPhone. Sarelio: Photo & Storage Cleaner does all three. It groups near-identical photos and bursts with on-device Vision, shows them side by side in Versus Mode so you keep the best one, and deletes nothing until you confirm on a review screen.

How do I delete duplicate photos on my iPhone?

iOS 16 and later has a built-in Duplicates album in Photos (Albums, then Utilities) that merges exact copies. For similar shots, bursts and near-duplicates, open Sarelio, go to Find, then Duplicates. Pick the best photo in each group, review, and confirm.

How do I free up storage on my iPhone without losing photos I care about?

Start with the biggest wins: large videos, screenshots, duplicates and blurry shots. In Sarelio, swipe right to trash and left to keep, then check everything once on the review screen before it's deleted. You can compress a big video instead of deleting it.

Is there a photo cleaner app that doesn't upload my photos?

Yes. Sarelio runs every scan on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to the cloud, there is no Sarelio account, and the app has no ad, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Is there a photo cleaner app without ads?

Sarelio has no ads anywhere: not on launch, not between swipes.

Does Sarelio delete photos automatically?

No. Swiping right only marks a photo. Nothing is deleted until you confirm on the review screen and then in the iOS prompt.

Can I undo a deletion?

Yes, twice over. Undo walks back your last swipes in the app. After you confirm, photos move to iOS Recently Deleted, where they stay for 30 days.

Why didn't my storage go up right after deleting?

Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted first. The space comes back when you empty that album in Photos or when iOS clears it after 30 days. Sarelio shows a reminder and an Open Photos button.

Does swipe direction flip in Hebrew or Arabic?

No. Right is always trash and left is always keep, in every language. Only the text is mirrored.

Which languages does Sarelio support?

English, Hebrew, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian and Arabic. It follows your device language.

Does Sarelio work with iCloud Photos?

Yes. Photos stored only in iCloud are downloaded by iOS for display, with a cloud badge and a Retry or Skip option when you're offline. Deleting a photo with iCloud Photos on also removes it from your other Apple devices.
