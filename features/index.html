Everything Sarelio does

Swipe to clean, plus finders that surface the clutter for you. Every one of them runs on your iPhone.

Storage full? Breathe.

See where your space goes. The Storage tab splits your iPhone by file type and album, and Home shows how much each cleanup can free before you start.

Swipe right to trash, left to keep

The same in every language, never mirrored. Swipe up to share, down to hide in the iOS Hidden album, double-tap to favorite. Buttons do the same if you prefer tapping.

Similar and duplicate photos, side by side

On-device Vision groups near-identical shots and bursts. Versus Mode puts them next to each other so you keep the best one.

Screenshot cleanup

Every screenshot in one place. Sarelio flags the ones with text, like codes and receipts, and can save them as PDF or text before you clear them.

Biggest videos first

Every video sorted by size, largest first. Preview, then delete, keep, or compress. Compress saves a smaller copy before the original goes to review.

Undo, then review, then delete

Multi-step Undo walks back your last decisions. Everything you trashed waits on one review screen, and deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Finders for the rest of the clutter

Blurry and too-dark photos, receipts, documents, RAW + JPEG pairs, Live Photos to stills, and folders from the Files app.

What's missing: ads, uploads, accounts

No ads. Nothing uploaded to the cloud. No Sarelio account. Analysis runs with iOS system frameworks on your iPhone.

  • Zen Mode

    A calm five-minute session with a leaf countdown, then a review prompt.

  • Shake to Clean

    Shake the phone and land on a random month from your past.

  • Hands-free voice cleaning

    Keep, delete and undo by voice, with on-device speech recognition.

  • Widgets

    Free space and your streak on the Home Screen, plus a widget that surfaces your blurriest recent photo.

  • Clean together

    Co-op cleaning with SharePlay in a FaceTime call.

  • 8 languages

    English, Hebrew, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian and Arabic, with full RTL.
